# Surface brief: danau.sk (all routes, home leads)

Scope: whole site redesign, home page leads. Visitor mode: Persuade (home, service pages), Read (legal pages).
Audience: public-sector and commercial decision makers looking for an external expert partner. Action: "Konzultovať potrebu" / contact form. Proof: real service copy, certifications; no invented stats, ratings or logos.
Constraints: copy verbatim from live danau.sk, same routes, colors of the live site, contact POST to /api/contact.php.
Direction: user-pinned (AutoBiz layout reference + pixel-mountain hero + live Danau colors). The roll was run (seed 0996b452) and is overridden by the pin; its star-atlas challenger donated one discipline (see raises).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Danau is the calm grid under complex IT change. The site refuses the stock consultancy hero (photo of handshake, blue gradient blob) and instead shows a field of data points assembling into mountains: many separate disciplines resolved into one landscape.

OWN-WORLD: Night ink #05061A grounds that warm into Danau blue #1818EC from below; pale-blue dot matrix (#93C5FD to #BFDBFE to white) as the single signature material, reused in hero, CTA band and footer. White and #F4F4F4 content grounds, ink #111827 text, gray #4B5563 copy, blue-tint #EEF icon chips. Light, airy Geist type at large sizes, tight tracking; generous whitespace; 20px-radius surfaces; pill buttons.

STORY: The visitor reads what DANAU does in one line, sees the six services as one family, understands how they work and for whom, and finishes on a contact path on every page.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Transparent nav over a near-black hero. H1 bottom-left at ~72px light, paragraph + white pill "Konzultovať potrebu" + outline "Naše služby" in the right column aligned to the H1 baseline. Lower half: canvas dot-matrix mountains rising out of a Danau-blue glow, full bleed.

FORM: user-pinned reference world (not from the ordered list); seed key 0996b452. Raise from star atlas: dot diameter on a fixed magnitude ramp carries height and light, never random noise. Raise from jacquard: one shared dot matrix drives every dot surface.

Signature interaction: mountains ignite bottom-up in a scan sweep on load, then shimmer; pointer acts as a lens that brightens nearby dots. Motion grammar: masked line reveals for headings, scroll-linked word highlighting for "Ako pracujeme?", sticky active list for "Pre koho pracujeme?", wordmark rising from the footer glow.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
